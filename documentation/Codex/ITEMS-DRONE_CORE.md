A deployable item that spawns a linked Drone when used on a block. Requires
HiveMind access first.

## Details

Right-click a block with a Drone Core to deploy a Drone above it, provided
there's enough clear space. The new Drone is automatically linked to you as
its owner and assigned a HiveCode (like `D-001`) for easy reference in
commands.

Drone Cores are consumed on use unless you're in creative mode. Each one
carries a subtle enchant glint to mark it as a HiveMind item.
